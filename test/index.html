Relume company timeline

January 2012

Co-founder Dan finishes the economics degree he promised his mum he would complete, only to never use it and start his own clothing business.

2012
June 2016

Adam catches wind of Dan's early success and slides into his DMs offering help. Dan politely accepts.

See the article that got Adam's attention
DM
August 2017

After collectively failing a number of business ventures, Dan and Adam finally have their first break. Together they build Lumio with a team of seven other people.

Lumio, previously called Foenix, was a social media analytics company that helped brands source legitimate influencers for campaigns.

April 2018

Lumio gets acquired by German company, Influencer DB.

Read about the acquisition
Exit
June 2018

Dan is shipped off to work with the German team as Head of Product at Influencer DB. Adam stays in Sydney and works as a product designer with a leading design studio.

Dan worked with brands such as Daniel Wellington and BMW. Adam worked with Vodafone, TAFE NSW, Adobe and won a Good Design Australia award.

Read about the Good Design Award
November 2019

After spending time apart, Dan and Adam begin to plan their next moves and rekindle their business partnership.

relume verb

January 2020

Dan moves back to Sydney to join a global startup incubator called Antler. He convinces Adam to join him.

Learn more about Antler
Antler
April 2020
Relume launches

For various reasons, Dan and Adam decide to turn down a $100,000 investment from Antler and start their own business venture without taking outside investment.

To do this they would have to build a company that would be profitable in its first year.

June 2020

After building 11 websites on Webflow in under six weeks to test business ideas, Dan and Adam decide to stick to what they know best and launch Relume.

11

Today

Today Relume is a team run by two people with the same mission.

To wake up every day with a choice to do what we love to do, build meaningful relationships, and do great work.
